Privacy Policy for BokeMon

Last Updated: 2026-04-22

BokeMon values your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data.

1. Image Data Processing

  • Local Processing: All image processing (text detection and blurring) is performed locally on your device using Apple's Vision and Core Image frameworks.
  • No Cloud Uploads: Your photos are never uploaded to any server or cloud service for processing.
  • Temporary Access: The app accesses your photo library only when you explicitly select a photo or save a processed image.

2. Data Collection

  • No Personal Information: BokeMon does not collect or store any personal information (names, email addresses, etc.).
  • Anonymous Usage Data: We may collect anonymous usage data (e.g., which features are used most) to improve the app, provided you have opted in via system settings.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Advertising: We use third-party advertising services (e.g., Google AdMob) to show ads. These services may collect device identifiers to provide relevant ads. Please refer to their respective privacy policies for details.

4. Contact
